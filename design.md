# Design direction

## An atlas of a restless mind
A visitor arrives at a threshold and travels through places that reveal how Sobi thinks and builds. The mood is an architectural exhibition after dark: monumental typography, restrained warm paper tones, precise marginal notes, and luminous environments.

The background is the atmosphere of the place. Content is placed with intention, not scattered as unexplained hotspots. Navigation uses plain language. The experience should reward curiosity without making someone solve a puzzle to find the work.

## Places
- Arrival: a monumental typographic threshold and doorway; an invitation to enter.
- Builder: an open work folio, with a project index and technical pages.
- AI Lab: an observatory with a selectable experiment and visibly labeled illustrative parameter studies.
- Research: an archive of expandable research records, like pulling a document from a shelf.
- Arsenal: a typographic tool cabinet grouped by discipline; selecting a tool reveals its use and relationships.
- Journey: a continuous chronological path through supplied milestones, with generous space between moments.
- About: a personal journal, warmer and quieter than the technical rooms.
- Beyond: an open horizon and a letter composer that hands off to the visitor's email application.

## Visual rules
Use large editorial titles, fine borders, generous negative space, and a small persistent atlas control. Avoid repeating a card grid or dashboard in every world. Preserve the existing background engines. Variations remain available through an atmosphere control. Mobile layouts become natural document flow rather than compressed desktop scenes.

## Motion
One short entrance animation per room. No scroll hijacking, forced waiting, autoplay sound, or cursor replacement. A still-mode control removes animated canvases and respects the system reduced-motion preference.
