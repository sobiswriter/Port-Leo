# Design direction

A galaxy of connected places: broad, atmospheric and lively, with professional names that make each destination understandable. Creative world names remain as a second layer of identity.

## Pages

- Universe: rotating 3D galaxy, drag-only camera orbit, scroll/pinch zoom, stars/gas/neighboring galaxies and an accessible destination directory.
- Overview: open typographic hero, followed by the professional introduction.
- Projects: collection of 16 works; title, plain live window, then content-sized project description. LaunchSILO, Wassap, Wat-EWrite and Shadow Diary lead.
- AI & Experiments: eight distinct AI studies with illustrative probes where available and four draft article previews.
- Research & Publications: six expandable editorial articles, warm paper, readable measure, source context and working-draft labels.
- Technical Skills: capability groups, selected skill inspector and related project links.
- Experience & Recognition: chronology of supplied milestones and recognition.
- About Me: personal writing, education/experience and a pinned Lanyard that can swing beyond its original card slot.
- Contact & Résumé: email/phone/social links, agency invitation, full-page PDF/text reader, open and download options.

## Visual and interaction rules

Heroes leave room for the environment; detailed content appears below. Use readable surfaces and meaningful interaction rather than decorative controls. Overview/Projects backgrounds have been gently dimmed for comfort. Live previews show only site content and open the site when clicked.

Galaxy journeys connect pages. The sound track attempts playback when ready and respects browser gesture requirements and mute. The owner’s rounded cursor and blue glow settings apply on suitable pointer devices. Reduced motion/still mode removes running page backgrounds and replaces the Lanyard with a keyboard-accessible static card. Content remains visible when motion is paused.

Mobile layouts use natural reading flow. Keyboard focus, semantic controls and source/direct links remain available; no hover-only route or content access is required.
